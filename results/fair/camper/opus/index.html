Questions,
answered

Still wondering? Ring the workshop on 01539 000 210 — you’ll speak to a builder, not a salesperson.

Is the configurator price final?

Yes. It includes VAT, the conversion, pre-delivery inspection and your first habitation service. The only extras are first registration and delivery if you don’t collect.

Can I change my build after reserving?

Up to six weeks before your build slot, you can change anything. Your price simply updates to whatever the configurator shows.

What does the deposit do?

It holds your build slot and locks today’s pricing for 90 days. It’s fully refundable until we order your chassis.

Why can’t I put a roof rack on a pop-top?

The pop-top lid has to lift freely, so we mount solar flush to the lid instead. Choose the high-top if you need rack space and a fixed tall roof.

Do you take part-exchange?

We do — tick the box when you reserve and we’ll send a valuation within two working days.
