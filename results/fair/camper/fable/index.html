Questions

Asked at every open day

Can I change the spec after I reserve?

Yes. Everything stays editable until the second week of your build, when we cut the roof and order the furniture panels. After that, add-ons can still be fitted, but roof and layout are fixed.

Is the price on this page the final price?

It is. The total includes VAT, registration and a full tank. Delivery to your door is quoted separately by distance. Collection from the workshop is free.

Why can’t I have a roof rack on the Pop-Top?

The elevating roof is rated for its own bed and two sleepers, not for cargo. Solar panels are fine because we use lightweight units bonded straight to the lid.

Can I finance it?

Yes. The monthly figure in the configurator assumes a 20% deposit over 84 months at 6.9% APR representative. We confirm a personal quote before you commit to anything.

Can I see one before I order?

The workshop is open on the first Saturday of every month, with one van in each roof type to climb around in. Bring the dog.
