The Flavian Amphitheatre

Inaugurated in AD 80, the Colosseum arranged its spectators in rising rings around an elliptical arena. Arched galleries carried crowds between the entrances and the seating tiers.

The hypogeum was added under Domitian. This interpretive model leaves part of the arena open so you can trace the underground corridors.
