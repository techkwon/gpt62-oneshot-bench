The Flavian Amphitheatre

Look through the arcade and into the bowl. The stepped seating wraps around an elliptical arena, while the surviving outer wall rises above it.

The cutaway reveals the hypogeum, a network of service passages beneath the wooden arena floor. Follow the parallel corridors to see how much of the spectacle happened out of sight.
