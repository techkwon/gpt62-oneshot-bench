Production / Proposed specifications

Two base colours unite four club accents.

Finished dimensions

Hard-enamel pin · 32 × 27 mm

Oval face, 1.5 mm thick. Use black nickel dividers at least 0.3 mm wide and a rubber clutch.

T-shirt print · 180 × 150 mm

Centre the plate on the chest, 70 mm below the collar. Scale to 150 × 125 mm on youth sizes.

Sew-on patch · 75 × 66 mm

Embroider on cream twill with a 3 mm forest merrowed edge. Keep stitched lines at least 1.2 mm wide.

Colour separations

Forest ink#203E34
Field cream#F8F1DC
Fox rust#BE5638
Workshop ochre#C18B43
Signal teal#508B90
Pond green#A1AC5E

Each mascot uses forest, cream and its own accent. Use solid fills and remove fine hatching on pins and patches.

On tees, let natural cotton supply the cream. Screen-print forest and the accent as two spot inks.

Proof at sizeMatch enamel, ink and thread samples to the palette in daylight before approving the run.
