Species in this guide

    Three formats per species

    Enamel pin

    The collectible. 32 mm of hard enamel on gold, sold on a printed backing card.

    T-shirt

    The club-day uniform. One front print, with the joke spelled out underneath.

    Backpack patch

    The everyday badge. Embroidered and sewn on, in a shape that belongs to the club.

    House rules

    1. Six spot colours across the range, and no more than four fills in any one design.
    2. One outline weight throughout, drawn heavy enough to survive a 32 mm pin.
    3. No lettering on pins or patches. The joke lives on the tee and the backing card.

    Seen together, the twelve pieces read as one collection with four clear identities

    Six spot colours and three standard sizes cover all twelve pieces

    Proposed dimensions

    Enamel pin
    32 mm on the longest side. Hard enamel on polished gold, metal lines at least 0.3 mm wide, backing card 50 × 70 mm.
    T-shirt
    280 mm maximum print width, top edge 70 mm below the collar. Screen print on Ink or Natural tees.
    Patch
    70 to 80 mm across. Embroidered on cream twill with a 3 mm merrowed border. Sew-on backing, no heat seal.
    Outline
    One weight on every piece: about 0.9 mm at pin size, scaled up in proportion on tees and patches.

    Colour instructions

      • Four fills per design plus the outline: Black 6 C on tees and patches, polished gold metal on pins.
      • On Ink tees, print a cream 7499 C underbase that runs 3 mm past the outline.
      • Patch borders take the club’s lead colour, stitched over cream twill.
      • Pantone numbers are nearest Solid Coated matches picked on screen. Check them against a swatch book at proof stage.
