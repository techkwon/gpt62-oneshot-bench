    Questions

    Before you
    commit

    Is the configurator price final?

    Yes. The total includes VAT, registration-ready paperwork and delivery to any mainland EU address. The only thing that can change it is something you add on the spec call.

    Can I drive it on a standard car licence?

    Every K1 is plated at 3,500 kg GVW. The payload readout in the configurator shows how much room is left for people, water and gear in your build.

    Pop-top or high roof?

    A pop-top keeps the van under 2.3 m, so it fits car parks and ferries at car rates, and it adds two berths upstairs. A high roof gives permanent standing room and better insulation for winter trips.

    What if I change my mind after reserving?

    Your €500 is refundable in full until the spec call. After that it becomes part of your deposit, and you can still change finishes up to four weeks before your build starts.
