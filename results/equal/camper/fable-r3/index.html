    Sheet 06 — Questions

    Before you commit.

    Is the deposit refundable?

    Yes, in full, until we order your base van — usually ten days after the spec call. After that it converts into your first stage payment.

    Can I change the spec after reserving?

    Paint, roof and layout lock when the van is ordered. Add-ons can be added or dropped until week four at the prices shown here.

    Will it fit where I need to park?

    The configurator shows the overall height of your exact build, including racks and lift. The low roof clears a 2.4 m barrier; the high roof needs 2.9 m.

    Do you deliver?

    Delivery to any address in the lower 48 is included. Most owners still choose to collect in Bend for the shakedown night.

    Can you convert a van I already own?

    If it’s the same chassis and under 30,000 miles, yes. Configure as normal and tell us on the spec call — we’ll deduct the base vehicle.

    How does payment work?

    $1,000 to reserve, 40% when the van arrives at the works, 40% at mid-build, and the balance at handover. Finance is available through two credit unions.
